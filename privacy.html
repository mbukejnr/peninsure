Our Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PenInsure may collect, use, store, and share personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or enquire about our pension and insurance brokerage services.

Important: This policy describes intended privacy practices and must be checked against PenInsure's actual operations before publication. It does not replace any additional privacy notices, contractual terms, or legal requirements that may apply to a particular pension or insurance arrangement.

01 Introduction

PenInsure ("we", "our", or "us") is presented on this website as a pension and insurance brokerage serving individuals, employers, businesses, and other organisations.

We recognise that personal information should be handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, communicate with us, or use our services.

Where we process personal information, we intend to do so for legitimate, clearly explained purposes and in accordance with applicable data-protection laws and other legal obligations.

Depending on the service, a pension administrator, insurer, employer, or another organisation may independently collect and process information under its own privacy notice and legal responsibilities.

02 Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with PenInsure and the services you request. It may include the following categories.

A. Personal and Contact Information

  • Full name and preferred form of address.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Postal address or other contact details where necessary.
  • Your preferred method of communication.

B. Business and Employer Information

  • Company or organisation name.
  • Business contact details and professional role.
  • Information about an organisation's pension or insurance enquiry.
  • Relevant scheme, workforce, or administrative information provided for a requested service.

C. Pension and Insurance Enquiry Information

If you ask us to assist with a pension or insurance matter, we may need information relevant to that request. Depending on the service, this could include scheme details, policy information, provider correspondence, requested coverage information, or documents needed to coordinate an enquiry.

Please do not send identity documents, financial account details, medical information, or other sensitive documents through a general website form unless we have confirmed an appropriate and secure method for doing so.

D. Website and Technical Information

Depending on the website's configuration and the services in use, technical information may include your browser type, device information, pages visited, approximate usage information, and security logs.

We will update this section if the website introduces analytics, tracking technologies, or other tools that collect additional information.

03 How We Collect Information

We may collect information through several channels, depending on the services and systems PenInsure uses.

  • Directly from you: when you complete a contact form, send an email, call us, or otherwise communicate with us.
  • Through your organisation: when an authorised employer or business representative provides information relevant to a requested service.
  • From relevant providers: where an insurer, pension administrator, or other service provider shares information appropriately in connection with your enquiry or an authorised arrangement.
  • Through website technology: where technical logs, cookies, or similar technologies are actually enabled.

Where information is provided on behalf of another person or organisation, the person supplying it should have the appropriate authority to do so.

04 How We Use Information

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the enquiry, personal information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Explaining pension and insurance brokerage services.
  • Coordinating authorised communications with relevant providers.
  • Assisting with requested documentation and administrative processes.
  • Managing client relationships and maintaining appropriate business records.
  • Supporting employer enquiries involving pension arrangements or insurance requirements.
  • Protecting the website, preventing misuse, and investigating security incidents.
  • Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, or record-keeping obligations.
  • Sending service-related updates where appropriate.

We will not treat consent as the only possible legal basis for every processing activity. Depending on the circumstances, applicable law may permit other lawful bases. Where consent is required, we will seek it as appropriate.

We will not use personal information for an unrelated purpose where doing so would be inconsistent with applicable law or the information provided to you.

05 When We May Share Information

PenInsure may need to share relevant information with other parties to respond to an enquiry or provide a requested brokerage service. Any sharing should be limited to what is appropriate for the relevant purpose and permitted by applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, recipients may include:

  • Insurers and pension providers: where information is required to obtain quotations, make enquiries, or coordinate a service requested by you.
  • Employers or authorised representatives: where necessary for a relevant business or employee-benefit arrangement and legally permitted.
  • Service providers: such as website hosting, communications, secure storage, or information-technology providers, if PenInsure uses them.
  • Professional advisers: where reasonably necessary for legal, compliance, accounting, or other professional support.
  • Public authorities: where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties. Any disclosure must be considered in light of the purpose, applicable legal requirements, and any relevant contractual or confidentiality obligations.

Pension providers, insurers, employers, and other recipients may process information under their own legal responsibilities and privacy policies.

06 Data Security

Personal information should be protected against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Depending on the systems and services in use, appropriate safeguards may include access controls, password protection, staff confidentiality obligations, secure communications, backups, and appropriate technical and organisational measures.

No website, email service, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid sending sensitive documents through ordinary email or a general contact form unless a suitable secure method has been confirmed.

Any actual security arrangements should be documented and maintained by PenInsure. This policy should not be interpreted as a claim that a particular security certification or technical safeguard is in place unless that has been verified.

07 How Long We Keep Information

We intend to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and record-keeping requirements.

Retention periods may differ for general enquiries, client correspondence, business records, pension or insurance documentation, and records that must be kept by law.

When information is no longer required, appropriate steps should be taken to delete it, securely dispose of it, or anonymise it, where appropriate and legally permitted.

PenInsure should establish and maintain a retention schedule suited to its actual operations rather than relying on an unspecified or indefinite retention period.

08 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the processing, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Ask whether personal information about you is being processed.
  • Request access to relevant personal information.
  • Ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion or restriction of processing where legally applicable.
  • Object to certain types of processing where the law provides that right.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on your consent.
  • Raise a concern or complaint with the relevant authority where applicable.

These rights are not absolute in every circumstance. Certain information may need to be retained or processed to meet legal obligations, establish or defend legal claims, or fulfil other lawful purposes.

To make a privacy-related request, contact PenInsure using the contact details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity and clarify your request before responding.

09 Cookies and Website Data

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. They can support essential website functions, remember preferences, or measure website usage.

The website may use essential technologies needed for its operation. Additional analytics or advertising technologies should only be described here if they are actually implemented.

If non-essential cookies or similar technologies are introduced, PenInsure should provide any required notices and consent controls under applicable law.

You may also be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions.

10 Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, including pension providers, insurers, or other organisations.

Those websites may have their own privacy policies, security arrangements, and terms of use. PenInsure does not control every external website and encourages visitors to review the relevant policies before submitting personal information.

If PenInsure uses external form-processing, analytics, hosting, or communication services, this policy should be updated to reflect the actual services used and any relevant data-sharing arrangements.

11 Children's Information

This website is primarily intended for adults, individuals seeking financial protection information, employers, businesses, and other organisations.

Where a pension or insurance enquiry concerns a child or another person who requires special protection under applicable law, information should be handled in accordance with the relevant legal requirements and any required authorisation.

Please contact us if you believe personal information has been submitted through the website in a way that requires review.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

PenInsure may update this policy to reflect changes in its services, website technology, information handling practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the revised policy should be published on this page with an updated effective date. Where required, additional notice or consent may be necessary.

Visitors should review this page periodically to understand the privacy practices currently described.
