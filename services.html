For Employers

Contribution Reconciliation Support

Employers may need to compare payroll schedules, expected pension contributions and recorded contributions. PenInsure can provide supporting reconciliation services to help identify differences that require further review.

  • Compare expected and recorded contributions
  • Review payroll-to-pension schedules
  • Identify contribution variances
  • Review employee-level differences
  • Identify outstanding or missing contributions
  • Prepare reconciliation summaries

Review Required
