Who We Are

About PenInsure

PenInsure is a pension and insurance brokerage focused on helping individuals, businesses and employers understand available options and navigate pension, retirement, annuity and insurance arrangements through relevant providers.

About Our Company

A brokerage focused on pensions and insurance.

PenInsure focuses on pension and insurance brokerage, helping clients understand their requirements, explore available options and coordinate with relevant providers.

Pension Brokerage

We support individuals and employers in exploring pension schemes, providers, retirement options and related pension arrangements.

Insurance Brokerage

We help clients explore insurance requirements, compare available quotations and coverage options, and coordinate with relevant insurers.

Retirement & Annuity Support

As part of pension brokerage, we support individuals exploring retirement income and annuity options available through relevant providers.

Our role: PenInsure provides brokerage and support services. Pension schemes, annuities, insurance policies and other financial products are provided by the relevant providers and remain subject to their terms, conditions and applicable requirements.

Retirement Support

Helping clients explore retirement & annuity options

Retirement and annuity support forms part of our pension brokerage services, particularly for individuals approaching or planning for retirement.

Retirement & Annuity Support

PenInsure can help clients understand and explore retirement income and annuity options that may be available through relevant providers.

This can include comparing available providers, understanding key features and coordinating enquiries or applications, subject to the relevant provider's terms and requirements.

Important: PenInsure acts as a broker and support intermediary. We do not represent ourselves as the provider of annuity products or retirement benefits.

What We Can Help You Explore

Depending on the relevant provider and available arrangements, our support may include:

  • Retirement income options
  • Annuity provider comparisons
  • Annuity product feature comparisons
  • Retirement benefit options
  • Pension transfer enquiries
  • Retirement and annuity documentation support
