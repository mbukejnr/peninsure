Explore Career Areas

Where Could You Contribute?

PenInsure's work brings together brokerage, client relationships, employer support and business operations. The following areas illustrate the types of skills that may be relevant to future recruitment.

Brokerage

Pension & Retirement Services

Support brokerage activities relating to pension schemes, retirement planning enquiries and annuity options available through relevant providers.

  • Pension scheme information and comparisons
  • Retirement and annuity enquiries
  • Client documentation and coordination
Brokerage

Insurance Brokerage

Contribute to client enquiries and the comparison and coordination of insurance options offered by relevant providers.

  • Insurance enquiries and documentation
  • Client relationship support
  • Application and renewal coordination
Corporate

Corporate & Employer Support

Support employers and businesses with pension-related enquiries, employee benefits coordination and client records.

  • Employer and business relationships
  • Employee benefits documentation
  • Reporting and administrative support
Client Service

Client Service & Administration

Help manage enquiries, maintain organised records and coordinate communication between clients and relevant providers.

  • Client enquiries and follow-ups
  • Records and document management
  • Communication and coordination
Business Support

Analysis & Reporting Support

Apply analytical and organisational skills to support business clients with relevant reconciliations, information and reporting.

  • Data review and reconciliation
  • Spreadsheet and reporting support
  • Accuracy and record management
Operations

Digital & Business Operations

Support the systems and processes that help a modern brokerage organise its information and communicate with clients.

  • Website and digital communication support
  • Business process improvement
  • Operational and technical support
